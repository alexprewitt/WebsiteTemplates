# Components Overview

## Header and navigation

- Branding header with logo and tagline
- Sticky navigation bar with active link highlighting
- Dedicated Book Appointment CTA
- Mobile collapse menu with menu-closing behavior

## Premium hero

- Split layout for hair and pet services
- Large editorial-style text and CTA buttons
- Overlapping image composition with soft accent tags

## Service selector

- Two large cards for hair and pet grooming
- Real anchor links to service sections
- Hover lift and image scale effects

## Hair and pet services

- Bootstrap card grid with consistent layout
- Different accent tones for each category
- Booking links for each service card

## Gallery

- Filterable gallery with category buttons
- Click-to-open modal for larger preview
- Optional before-and-after slider for a performance-focused showcase

## Team

- Three profile cards with placeholder portraits
- Simple hover elevation and readable layout
- Easy to remove or expand for more staff members

## Testimonials

- Three sample testimonial cards
- Clear placeholder labeling in the source and docs
- No fake star ratings or review platform claims

## FAQ

- Bootstrap accordion
- Generic example questions and responses
- Easy to customize if the business adopts specific policies

## Booking form

- Form fields for customer, service, date, and notes
- Booking option buttons that preselect the appointment type
- Demo-only submission state with no backend integration

## Contact section

- Phone, email, address, and hours
- Directory-style contact details and social links
- Studio image or location placeholder

## Footer and back-to-top

- Dark premium footer with accent text
- Quick navigation and legal placeholders
- Floating back-to-top button for keyboard and mouse users
