Aftercare

Great Tattoos Deserve Great Care.

  • Follow your artist's preparation instructions and confirm appointment details.
  • Discuss any medication, medical concerns, or relevant questions with appropriate professionals.
  • Arrive well-rested and prepared to discuss your design and placement.

  • Follow the artist-provided written aftercare guidance for your specific tattoo.
  • Keep the tattoo clean according to professional instructions and avoid unnecessary irritation.
  • Watch for any unexpected changes and seek appropriate medical evaluation if needed.

The artist's official aftercare instructions and relevant medical guidance take precedence over general advice. If a tattoo shows signs of infection or other concerning symptoms, consult a qualified medical professional.
